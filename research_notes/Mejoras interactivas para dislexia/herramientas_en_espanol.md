# Spanish-language digital tools for early readers (6–9) with dyslexia or reading difficulties

Scope note: research done 2026-10-14 with web search and fetch, about 21 tool calls. Most detail comes from vendor pages and press, and is marked as such. Two academic PDFs (the 2024 Revista de Educación review of dyslexia videogames, and the UGR Galexia note) were downloaded but their text could not be extracted, so their contents are not used beyond search snippets.

## 1. Per tool: target age, skills trained, concrete activities

### Takeaway
Only a handful of Spanish tools publish activity-level detail. The well-documented ones are Dytective/DytectiveU, Piruletras/Dyseggxia, Galexia, Glifing/ReadUp, Leo con Grin, Smartick Lectura and Pipo. Lexiland, Árbol ABC, Leo Lo Que Veo, Lectópolis and Mundo Primaria returned no reliable sources in this session (see Gaps). A 2024 Revista de Educación review lists 8 dyslexia videogames sold in Spain with some scientific evidence: Galexia, Ubinding, Glifing, Tradislexia, GraphoGame, Jellys, DytectiveU and Minecraft.

### Cited Findings

**Dytective (screening) / DytectiveU (training). Change Dyslexia, Luz Rello**
- It is a digital tool to improve reading and writing, with a screening test for dyslexia risk and other literacy difficulties. It is funded by EduCaixa (Fundación "la Caixa") — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/)
- It trains 24 cognitive skills in 5 groups. Linguistic (8): alphabetic, phonological awareness, syllabic, lexical, morphological, syntactic, semantic, orthographic. Working memory (4): visual, visual-sequential, auditory, auditory-sequential. Executive functions (3): sustained, selective and divided attention. Performance (7): reading comprehension, reading speed, natural spelling, arbitrary spelling, writing speed, error recognition, error correction. Perceptual (2): visual and auditory discrimination and categorization — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/)
- It has more than 42,000 adaptive activities. The number differs by source: other sources say 35,000 or 40,000 — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/); [blogthinkbig search summary](https://blogthinkbig.com/detectar-la-dislexia-a-traves-del-juego)
- It is available from age 5, on iOS, Android and web — search summary of [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/article/id/413178-science-at-the-service-of-dyslexia-with-customised-transformational-tools)
- Concrete screening activities:
  - **Letter or syllable search:** the child hears a target letter or syllable, then has 15 seconds to find it among many letters or syllables on screen.
  - **Sentence error detection:** the child finds the errors in written sentences.
  - **Scoring:** points are earned for fast completion. — [Blog Think Big](https://blogthinkbig.com/detectar-la-dislexia-a-traves-del-juego)
- The screening takes about 15 minutes, and a professional must confirm any result — [Blog Think Big](https://blogthinkbig.com/detectar-la-dislexia-a-traves-del-juego); [Pervasive Health 2016 paper](https://www.changedyslexia.org/publications/pdfs/2016-Pervasive%20Health-Dytective%20Detecting%20Risk%20of%20Dyslexia.pdf)

**Piruletras (Spanish name) / Dyseggxia (English name). Rello, Bayarri, Gorriz**
- Its word exercises are built from an analysis of real writing errors made by children with dyslexia. Instead of showing the correct word, the game shows wrong words that the child must detect or fix. Letter and syllable transpositions make up only about 2% of real errors, so the game does not focus on them — [Hispadoc interview](https://hispadoc.es/descarga/articulo/5085472.pdf); [UPF repository, "Design of word exercises for children with dyslexia", Procedia CS 2014](https://repositori.upf.edu/items/78d49611-f29b-451c-849a-5df5db7fa28b)
- The game has 5 exercise types and 3 levels (easy, medium, hard). The types are:
  - **Omission:** a letter is missing from the word.
  - **Insertion:** the word has an extra letter.
  - **Substitution:** one letter has been replaced by a wrong one.
  - **Correct ending:** the child chooses the right word ending.
  - **Word split:** the child separates words that were run together. — [Hispadoc](https://hispadoc.es/descarga/articulo/5085472.pdf)
- Exercises are designed in 6 stages: exercise type, word selection, word modification, distractor selection, difficulty levels, text layout. Difficulty depends on word frequency, word length and orthographic similarity to other words (neighbors). More than 5,000 exercises exist in Spanish and English — [UPF repository](https://repositori.upf.edu/items/78d49611-f29b-451c-849a-5df5db7fa28b); [wwwhatsnew](https://wwwhatsnew.com/?p=139842)

**Galexia. Universidad de Granada (Defior's group)**
- It is aimed at primary grades 2 to 6. The child travels with an alien through space over 24 game sessions toward its planet, "Leximundo", solving puzzles and riddles and fighting enemy UFOs — [psiquiatria.com](https://psiquiatria.com/trastornos-infantiles-y-de-la-adolescencia/cientificos-de-la-ugr-desarrollan-una-app-que-mejora-notablemente-la-fluidez-lectora-en-ninos-con-dislexia/); [Valencia Plaza](https://valenciaplaza.com/valenciaplaza/disenan-una-app-que-mejora-la-fluidez-lectora-de-ninos-con-dislexia)
- It uses repeated reading and accelerated reading. Content progresses from syllables to words to texts — [Caderno de Letras 2022](https://periodicos.ufpel.edu.br/index.php/cadernodeletras/article/download/21911/14225); [psiquiatria.com](https://psiquiatria.com/trastornos-infantiles-y-de-la-adolescencia/galexia-mejora-la-fluidez-lectora-51064/)
- It was released for Android only, around 2016, and was nominated for Best University Game at the 2016 Premios Nacionales del Videojuego — [Valencia Plaza](https://valenciaplaza.com/valenciaplaza/disenan-una-app-que-mejora-la-fluidez-lectora-de-ninos-con-dislexia)

**Glifing / ReadUp by Glifing. Barcelona, created by psychologist Montserrat Garcia**
- Programs: Infantil 4 and 5 years, primary grades 1 to 3, and teens and adults. Available in Spanish, Catalan and English — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/)
- It is a phonological method: grapheme to phoneme, then syllable, then word. The main target is reading speed and fluency — [Hola](https://www.hola.com/padres/20240530360776/metodo-glifing-lectura-autismo/); [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/)
- The ReadUp app is for ages 6 to 12, in Spanish and Catalan. It is organized in reading levels, then islands, each island with 30 missions. A dragon accompanies the child — [App Store, ReadUp](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1384459768)
- Named games: "Trabalenguas" (tongue twisters), "Comprensión de metáforas", "Descodificación", "Dinosaurios", "Atención". It also lists syllable segmentation, vocabulary, categorization, definitions and logical reasoning — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/)
- Concrete activities:
  - **Name the pictures:** three images are shown and the child names each one.
  - **Which one disappears:** the child memorizes the three images and says which one will disappear (working memory).
  - **Literal or figurative:** the child reads a sentence and picks between a literal and a figurative meaning, for example "Pedro tiene el pelo de oro".
  - **Dilemma dialogues:** the child hears friends give opinions on what to do, says which opinion is right and explains why. — [Hola](https://www.hola.com/padres/20240530360776/metodo-glifing-lectura-autismo/)

**Leo con Grin / Aprender a leer con Grin 1 and 2. Educaplanet**
- Part 1 is for ages 4 to 6. It has 30 lessons grouped in 6 missions: vowels first, then consonants, with syllables, words and sentences — [Educación 3.0](https://www.educaciontrespuntocero.com/recursos/aprender-leer-infantil-la-app-leo-grin/); [BlueStacks listing via search](https://www.bluestacks.com/es/apps/educational/i-read-with-grin-on-pc.html)
- Named minigames:
  - **CRABS:** form syllables from letters.
  - **BUTTERFLIES:** identify syllables.
  - Others: form words from syllables, read words and sentences. — [Xataka / app listing via search](https://www.xatakamovil.com/aplicaciones/cinco-aplicaciones-gratis-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando)
- Writing practice: the child picks a letter (upper or lower case) and works through tracing, then copying with help, then free writing — [Educación 3.0](https://www.educaciontrespuntocero.com/recursos/aprender-leer-infantil-la-app-leo-grin/)
- Part 2 ("Grin y Uipi") is for ages 5 to 8, with 25 lessons. It covers consonant clusters (trabadas) and compound syllables, word and sentence reading, and comprehension. Games include:
  - **Robot:** presents a word and its parts.
  - **Asteroide:** the child fits each syllable into its matching shape. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/id1438493204)

**Smartick Lectura**
- It is for ages 4 to 14 and targets precision, speed, intonation and comprehension. The sequence is:
  1. Pre-alphabetic preparation.
  2. Letter-sound links taught through stories and games.
  3. Automatized syllable and word reading.
  4. Punctuation.
  5. Comprehension at word, sentence and text level. — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/smartick-no-solo-matematicas-su-nuevo-programa-lectura-sirve-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando); [ImpactoTIC](https://impactotic.co/innovacion/educacion/una-buena-lectura-es-indispensable-para-el-exito-de-los-procesos-de-aprendizaje-smartick/)

**Aprende a leer con Pipo / Vamos a leer con Pipo (Cibal)**
- Level 1 is for ages 3 to 6. It has 18 games for visual discrimination, memory, shape recognition, association and vocabulary. Completing each letter earns a printable diploma to color. It was released in 1996 for Windows. "Vamos a leer con Pipo" (ages 5 to 8) covers syllables, words and sentences — [EcuRed](https://www.ecured.cu/Aprende_a_leer_con_Pipo_1)

**Latin America**
- Chile's BDEscolar (Mineduc) is a free digital library for subsidized schools, with ebooks and audiobooks. It has an accessibility module with text-to-audio and a dyslexia-friendly reading option, and content is profiled by age — [Ayuda Mineduc](https://ayudamineduc.cl/ficha/biblioteca-digital-escolar)
- Colombia: "JuegAprende" is a thesis prototype for ages 8 to 10, working on lexical and semantic processes through game-based learning — [UPN repository via search](https://repositorio.upn.edu.co/bitstreams/9e1f6d70-eae0-485b-9d78-4b17008c50fd/download)
- Dytective's screening validation included schools in Chile and Colombia. A study with Fundación Piñera Morel in Chile is reported — [Blog Think Big](https://blogthinkbig.com/detectar-la-dislexia-a-traves-del-juego); [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/)

**Associations and resource lists**
- A Universitat de València resource guide lists these reference sites: Disfam, FEDIS, ladislexia.net, Katamotz, Magia Lectora and Soy Visual. Its sections cover worksheets, software, diagnostic tests and stories — [CRIE UV PDF](https://crie.blogs.uv.es/files/2023/04/La-dislexia-recursos-y-materiales.pdf)
- For primary cycle 1, Educación 3.0 recommends TTS readers (Claroread, TTSReader, Narakeet) and Genially, Canva and Live Worksheets — [Educación 3.0](https://www.educaciontrespuntocero.com/opinion/herramientas-primaria-dislexia/)

### Inferences
- The two most "reimplementable" activity families are these:
  - Rello's error-detection exercises: omission, insertion and substitution, with distractors chosen by frequency, length and orthographic neighbors.
  - Glifing and Galexia's repeated and accelerated reading of syllables, then words, then text.
- The Grin "letters → syllables → words → sentences" sequence with tracing, copying and free writing is the standard Spanish syllabic progression. It suits grades 1 to 2.

### Gaps
- **Lexiland:** two searches found no tool with this name. It may not exist, or it may have little web presence. It may be confused with Galexia.
- **Árbol ABC, Leo Lo Que Veo, Lectópolis, Mundo Primaria, Cuadernia/Educaplay dyslexia resources:** searches returned nothing reliable. Their status in 2026 is unverified, not confirmed discontinued.
- **Mexico (SEP) and El Salvador (MINED):** no dedicated early-reading or dyslexia platform was found.
- **Lexia in Spanish:** not found.
- DytectiveU's detailed in-game exercise list is not described on the vendor page.
- The 2024 Revista de Educación review was not readable (PDF extraction failed) — [recyt.fecyt.es](https://recyt.fecyt.es/index.php/Redu/article/download/100333/78991/428069)

## 2. Difficulty adaptation and error feedback

### Takeaway
Adaptation is mostly an initial placement test plus automatic level adjustment. Only Rello's tools document error-type modelling (omission, insertion, substitution) built from real dyslexic errors. No source found here describes specific b/d or p/q confusion handling.

### Cited Findings
- DytectiveU's intervention materials adjust automatically to each student's level, based on the child's strengths and weaknesses — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/)
- Piruletras modelled difficulty on word frequency, word length and orthographic similarity, then added computational adaptation per child — [wwwhatsnew](https://wwwhatsnew.com/?p=139842); [Hispadoc](https://hispadoc.es/descarga/articulo/5085472.pdf)
- Glifing starts with a placement test of speed, accuracy and comprehension, compared against grade and age norms reviewed at the Universidad de Barcelona. The result is an individualized plan, and progress is logged so professionals can adjust it — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/)
- Smartick asks for age and any special educational needs at sign-up, gives a placement test, then uses AI to adjust pace — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/smartick-no-solo-matematicas-su-nuevo-programa-lectura-sirve-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando)
- Leo con Grin has two levels that can be combined. Level 2 adds inverse syllables, adds more items and removes the help in the instruction (scaffold fading) — [Educación 3.0](https://www.educaciontrespuntocero.com/recursos/aprender-leer-infantil-la-app-leo-grin/)
- Galexia's materials are adapted to the child's academic level, in an individualized, sequential progression — [Galexia search summary / UGR](https://secretariageneral.ugr.es/print/pdf/node/15223)
- Glifing's activities repeat and are systematic, difficulty rises gradually, and the screen shows how many activities are done and how many remain — [Hola](https://www.hola.com/padres/20240530360776/metodo-glifing-lectura-autismo/)

### Inferences
- A good pattern to copy: tag each item by error type, then raise or lower difficulty along frequency, length and neighbor axes.

### Gaps
- No source describes in-the-moment corrective feedback (retry, hint, audio model) for any tool.
- No source documents explicit b/d or p/q confusion targeting.

## 3. Motivation mechanics and session length

### Takeaway
Most tools converge on short sessions, about 15 to 20 minutes, 4 days a week, inside a narrative world (space travel, islands with a dragon companion, missions) with light collectible rewards.

### Cited Findings
- DytectiveU: 4 sessions a week of about 15 minutes — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/). Its research study used 20 minutes, 4 days a week, for 8 weeks — [search summary of CORDIS/UCM](https://www.ucm.es/tribunacomplutense/172/art2341.php)
- Dytective screening scores points for speed — [Blog Think Big](https://blogthinkbig.com/detectar-la-dislexia-a-traves-del-juego)
- Glifing: the vendor says 20 minutes a day, 4 days a week — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/). Press reports 4 sessions of 15 minutes a week — [Diario de León/EFE](https://www.diariodeleon.es/sociedad/230903/531726/videojuego-hecho-barcelona-ayuda-leer-ninos-20-paises.html). ReadUp uses islands of 30 missions each, with a dragon companion — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1384459768)
- Galexia: 24 sessions as a space journey with an alien, with puzzles, riddles and enemy UFOs — [psiquiatria.com](https://psiquiatria.com/trastornos-infantiles-y-de-la-adolescencia/cientificos-de-la-ugr-desarrollan-una-app-que-mejora-notablemente-la-fluidez-lectora-en-ninos-con-dislexia/)
- Leo con Grin: fruit prizes earned per solved exercise, missions, and child avatars (up to 3 profiles) — [Educación 3.0](https://www.educaciontrespuntocero.com/recursos/aprender-leer-infantil-la-app-leo-grin/)
- Smartick: at most 15 minutes a day, done alone, with memory and attention games at the end of the session as a reward — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/smartick-no-solo-matematicas-su-nuevo-programa-lectura-sirve-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smartick-kids-learn-math/id823586852?l=vi)
- Pipo: a printable diploma to color after each letter — [EcuRed](https://www.ecured.cu/Aprende_a_leer_con_Pipo_1)
- Piruletras: 12 sessions of 20 minutes in its evaluation — [Hispadoc via search](https://hispadoc.es/descarga/articulo/5085472.pdf)

### Inferences
- About 15 minutes, 4 times a week, is the de facto Spanish market standard. A visible "activities left" counter plus a narrative journey is the common motivation shell.

### Gaps
- No source found describes streaks or daily-login mechanics.
- Typography choices are not documented in the sources found: for example Rello's research recommendations on sans-serif fonts, larger size and spacing, or Dytective's colors.
- TTS and audio use is only implied (the child hears the target letter in Dytective; Pipo and Grin use voice). It is not described in detail.

## 4. What teachers and parents get

### Takeaway
Dashboards are mainly progress tracking. Dytective adds group screening for schools, Glifing adds norm-referenced reader profiles, and Grin offers per-child accuracy and completion.

### Cited Findings
- Dytective: teachers see real-time results and automatic progress reports, and families follow progress from home. Screening is easy to apply to many students at once, and 460,855 people have used it since December 2016 — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/)
- Glifing: a reader profile (speed, accuracy, comprehension) compared against grade norms, an individualized plan, and progress logs for professionals. There are separate tracks for clinics (gabinetes), schools and families — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/)
- Leo con Grin: per-child view of correct and incorrect answers and the percentage of exercises completed — [Educación 3.0](https://www.educaciontrespuntocero.com/recursos/aprender-leer-infantil-la-app-leo-grin/)
- Smartick: tutors get daily progress information — [BioBio Chile via search](https://www.biobiochile.cl/especial/e-learning/noticias/2020/11/13/smartick-la-plataforma-que-ayuda-a-los-ninos-a-reforzar-matematicas.shtml)
- Pricing and model, briefly:

  | Tool | Pricing / model |
  |---|---|
  | Dytective | Free for Spanish schools via EduCaixa. Families pay €99 per semester. Free for therapists. Scholarships or licenses outside Spain. — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/) |
  | Glifing | No public prices. Free demo. — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/) |
  | Smartick | 7-day free trial. — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/smartick-no-solo-matematicas-su-nuevo-programa-lectura-sirve-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando) |
  | Leo con Grin | Freemium: vowels and the letter L are free, the other 28 lessons are paid. — [Xataka](https://www.xatakamovil.com/aplicaciones/cinco-aplicaciones-gratis-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando) |
  | BDEscolar | Free for Chilean subsidized schools. — [Ayuda Mineduc](https://ayudamineduc.cl/ficha/biblioteca-digital-escolar) |

### Gaps
- No screenshots or field-level dashboard specs were found for any tool, such as which charts are shown or which recommendations are given.

## 5. Published evidence of effectiveness

### Takeaway
Evidence is strongest, though still mostly from the developer team, for Dytective/DytectiveU and Piruletras. Glifing's evidence is mainly vendor claims plus a tiny pilot. Galexia's is press releases plus small studies. Smartick has no reading-specific evidence found.

### Cited Findings
- **DytectiveU, research study:** 8 weeks, 20 minutes, 4 days a week, alongside speech therapy. The experimental group had a significantly larger drop in dyslexia risk and gains in phoneme segmentation, pseudoword reading and verbal fluency — [search summary, UCM Tribuna](https://www.ucm.es/tribunacomplutense/172/art2341.php)
- **Dytective, vendor-reported results:**
  - More than 40% reduction in the global dyslexia-risk coefficient in 6 months, measured with DST-J.
  - A study of 13,406 students in 107 Madrid schools (with LSE, UCL and URJC).
  - A study of 600 children in Chile with Exeter and UCL, reporting gains in confidence, motivation and national language test results.
  - The underlying data is not shown on the page. — [changedyslexia.org](https://www.changedyslexia.org/)
- **Dytective screening:** about 90% reported accuracy — [Blog Think Big](https://blogthinkbig.com/detectar-la-dislexia-a-traves-del-juego); [Pervasive Health 2016](https://www.changedyslexia.org/publications/pdfs/2016-Pervasive%20Health-Dytective%20Detecting%20Risk%20of%20Dyslexia.pdf)
- **Piruletras:** 48 children with dyslexia aged 6 to 11, at a Barcelona school with the Creix center. After 8 weeks (12 sessions of 20 minutes) they made 42% fewer errors. No control group was reported — [Hispadoc](https://hispadoc.es/descarga/articulo/5085472.pdf)
- **Glifing:** the vendor claims more than 95% of children improve one reading grade in 4 to 6 months, and "1 curso lector en 5 meses". Its pilot with children with Down syndrome had n=4 experimental and n=2 control — [glifing.com](https://www.glifing.com/); [Estudio piloto PDF](https://www.glifing.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Estudio-Glifing-SD.pdf)
- **Galexia:** press reports "notable" fluency gains. It was tested in Spain across ages and profiles — [psiquiatria.com](https://psiquiatria.com/trastornos-infantiles-y-de-la-adolescencia/galexia-mejora-la-fluidez-lectora-51064/); [Caderno de Letras 2022](https://periodicos.ufpel.edu.br/index.php/cadernodeletras/article/download/21911/14225). Ecuadorian school use is described in a LATAM Revista 2024 article — [redilat](https://revistalatam.redilat.org/index.php/lt/article/view/2917)
- **Smartick:** the only outcome evidence found is for math, not reading — [search summary](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/smartick-no-solo-matematicas-su-nuevo-programa-lectura-sirve-ninos-aprendan-a-leer-jugando)

### Gaps
- No independent RCT was found for any Spanish tool in this session.
- Effect sizes for Galexia were not retrieved.
- 2026 availability was not verified for Galexia (Android-only, 2016), Piruletras (iOS, older) or Pipo (1996 Windows software, likely legacy).
